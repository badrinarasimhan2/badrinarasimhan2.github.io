RESEARCH VISION

From matrix dynamics to biological organization.

Cells do not experience static environments. My research seeks to define how dynamic extracellular cues are sensed, integrated, and translated into changes in cell behavior and tissue organization.

These principles are then reconstructed in engineered microenvironments to create predictive and controllable models of complex biological systems.

ABOUT

Portrait

Badri Narayanan Narasimhan, Ph.D.

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in Bioengineering at the University of California, San Diego.

My research investigates how extracellular matrix remodeling regulates cellular organization, mechanosensation, and disease progression. I integrate engineered biomaterials, mechanobiology, quantitative modeling, and spatial profiling to understand how matrix mechanics, adhesion, and degradation shape cellular states.

My research trajectory spans dissipative hydrogel design, collagen microarchitecture and degradation, degradation-induced stress relaxation, and the reconstruction of disease-relevant microenvironments.
